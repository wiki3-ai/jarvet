JARVET.AIHere's how school gets paid for:
  • Post-9/11 GI Bill — tuition paid directly to the school, plus a monthly housing allowance and a yearly book stipend.
  • Yellow Ribbon Program — covers the gap when tuition runs higher than the GI Bill cap, common at private or out-of-state schools.
  • VR&E (Chapter 31) — for a service-connected disability rating, can fund training plus a monthly subsistence allowance.
  • Think your GI Bill is gone? You can file an appeal requesting your benefits be extended — a lot of vets never realize this is an option, and it's worth checking before assuming the door is closed.
